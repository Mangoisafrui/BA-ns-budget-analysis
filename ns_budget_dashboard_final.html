Finance & Treasury Board Note

The 2025–26 deficit reflects a $200M contingency reserve introduced to address potential US tariff impacts on the Canadian economy, unanticipated emergency events, and emerging fiscal year priorities. The underlying operating deficit before contingency is –$697.5M, driven primarily by a 6.4% increase in departmental expenditures — concentrated in Health & Wellness (+$440M), Education (+$227M), and Public Works (+$124M).

Fiscal Guardrail Context

The Province's fiscal planning guardrail sets a ceiling of 40% Net Debt-to-GDP. At 34.6%, the Province maintains a 5.4 percentage point buffer. While Net Debt is projected to grow to $29.0B by 2028–29, nominal GDP growth of ~4–5% annually is expected to keep the ratio within guardrail limits throughout the four-year plan horizon, provided economic conditions remain stable.
